How to play

  1. Everyone stands up.
  2. Teacher presses Next Action.
  3. “Simon says …” → do the action.
  4. No “Simon says” → do not move!
  5. Press Reveal to check.

Remember

Exercise keeps us strong and energetic. Yoga improves our posture. After a lot of play, our body needs rest and good sleep.
