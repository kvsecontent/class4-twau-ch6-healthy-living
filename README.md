# Happy and Healthy Living – Games (Class 4 TWAU, Chapter 6)

Interactive smartboard games for NCERT Class 4 *Our Wondrous World* Chapter 6 **Happy and Healthy Living** (topics: *Enjoy Sports* and *Sleep Well too!*).

**Enjoy Sports**
- **Games Wheel** – spin the wheel (Kho-kho, Kabaddi, Skipping, Cycling, Lagori, Cricket), name the game and choose the body part that moves the most. Two teams.
- **Indoor or Outdoor?** – drag 16 game cards into the Indoor Games or Outdoor Games box.
- **Simon Says** – 16 action cards with “Simon says” chance, read-aloud and timer.

**Sleep Well too!**
- **Sleep Clock** – drag the hour hands for bedtime and wake-up time; the hours of sleep are counted step by step (links with Maths – time). Includes question mode.
- **How Do I Feel?** – Fresh / Lazy / Sleepy situation quiz (textbook sleep-table feelings a, b, c) plus “Sleepy Bear” mime mode.
- **My Sleep Chart** – class sleep table (sleep time, wake-up time, duration, feeling) with automatic duration and summary; saved in the browser.
- **Day–Night Routine** – put 7 routine cards in order from waking up to sleeping.

**Recap**
- **Healthy Living Puzzle** – join Good Food, Exercise and Sound Sleep to make “Happy & Healthy Me”.
- **Good Habit or Bad Habit?** – 12 picture cards; class shows thumbs up / thumbs down.

Single self-contained `index.html` — no internet libraries needed.
